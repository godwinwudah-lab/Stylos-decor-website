Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Stylo's Decor & Painting Works ("we", "us" or "our") handles information when you visit https://stylodecor.netlify.app or contact us about our painting and decorative finishing services.

1. Information you choose to share

If you contact us by phone or WhatsApp, you may choose to share your name, contact details, property or project location, photographs, and details about the work you need. We use that information to respond to your enquiry, discuss your requirements, and prepare or follow up on a quotation.

Our website's quotation link may open WhatsApp with a message for you to review and send. Information you send through WhatsApp is handled by WhatsApp under its own terms and privacy policy.

2. Website and device information

When you visit a website, your browser or hosting provider may process technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device information, pages requested, and visit time for delivery, security, and troubleshooting. We do not ask you to submit sensitive personal information through this website.

3. Cookies, advertising and third-party services

This website may use third-party services such as website hosting, fonts, images, analytics, or advertising services. Those providers may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own privacy policies.

If Google AdSense or another advertising service is enabled on this website, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure, and personalise advertisements, subject to applicable settings and consent requirements. You can learn more about Google's advertising practices at Google's Advertising page and its general privacy policy at Google's Privacy Policy.

We do not control the cookies or data practices of third-party services. Please review their privacy notices for more information.

4. How we use information

We use information you provide to answer enquiries, communicate about painting and decorating services, prepare quotations, arrange site visits, and improve the way we present our services. We do not sell your contact details to third parties.

5. Sharing and protection

We may rely on service providers to host or operate parts of the website and communication services. We do not publish private enquiry details. We take reasonable steps to handle information carefully, but no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Your choices

You can choose not to submit personal information through the website or WhatsApp. You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Some third-party advertising or analytics services may provide their own privacy and personalisation controls.

7. Children's privacy

This website is intended for people seeking information about our services and is not directed at children. Please do not send us a child's personal information unless it is necessary for an enquiry and you are authorised to do so.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
