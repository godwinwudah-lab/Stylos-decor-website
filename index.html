What We Do

Our Services

Specialised in professional wall finishes and ceiling solutions for residential and commercial projects.

Wall & Structure Painting

Complete interior and exterior wall painting. Proper surface preparation, quality materials and clean, even finishes that last.

Grafiato Painting

Textured grafiato finishes that add depth and character to walls. Available in a range of patterns and colours to suit modern and classic interiors.

Wall Treatments

Surface preparation, repairs, sealing and specialised wall treatments. We fix cracks, uneven surfaces and prepare walls properly before finishing.

Plasterboard Ceilings

Supply and installation of plasterboard (drywall) ceilings. Smooth, level finishes ready for paint, with attention to detail on joints and edges.
